# How-To Guide Profile

*Read when helping a competent reader complete a real task or recover from a known operational problem.*

## Controlling Claim

Establish that the documented procedure produces one stated result under defined prerequisites and conditions.

## Reasoning Obligations

- Name the concrete result before the steps.
- State prerequisites, permissions, inputs, and destructive effects before the action that depends on them.
- Order steps by execution dependency.
- Use imperative language and exact commands, fields, or controls where correctness requires them.
- Give expected outputs or checkpoints that let readers verify progress.
- Explain branches at the point where conditions diverge.
- Provide recovery or troubleshooting for likely failure states.

## Default Progression

Move from result and prerequisites through the procedure, checkpoints, conditional branches, verification, and recovery.

## Exclude by Default

Do not interrupt the task with extended architecture history or conceptual background. Link a technical explanation when readers need the deeper model.

## Completion Check

A prepared reader should be able to perform the task, determine whether each critical stage succeeded, and recover from the documented failure states.
