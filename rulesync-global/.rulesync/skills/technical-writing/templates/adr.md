# Architecture Decision Record Profile

*Read when recording one architecturally significant decision for future maintainers.*

## Controlling Claim

Establish that one decision responds to a specific set of technical, organizational, or project pressures and changes the future context in understood ways.

## Reasoning Obligations

- Describe the relevant context and competing forces without loading the description in favor of the decision.
- State the decision in complete, active language.
- Record its lifecycle status and any decision it supersedes.
- Explain why the recorded pressures selected it.
- Preserve positive, negative, and neutral consequences.
- Link related decisions when one consequence becomes another ADR's context.

## Default Progression

Use a short title followed by context, decision, status, and consequences. Add alternatives only when they clarify the forces or prevent future readers from reopening a settled path without new evidence.

## Exclude by Default

Do not expand one ADR into a complete system overview or implementation plan. Link those documents when they exist.

## Completion Check

A future maintainer should understand what changed, why the decision held under its original context, which costs the team accepted, and whether the decision remains active.
