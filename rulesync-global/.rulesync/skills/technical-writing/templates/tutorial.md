# Tutorial Profile

*Read when guiding a learner through a safe, successful experience that develops a specific capability.*

## Controlling Claim

Establish that completing one carefully bounded experience teaches the learner how the central concepts work together.

## Reasoning Obligations

- State what the learner will build or accomplish and which knowledge the tutorial assumes.
- Choose a result small enough to complete successfully while exercising the intended concepts.
- Introduce one meaningful concept at the moment the learner uses it.
- Sequence actions so each stage produces a visible or verifiable result.
- Protect the learner from irrelevant choices and unsupported branches.
- Explain only enough theory to make the current action meaningful, then link deeper explanations.
- End by connecting the completed experience to the capability the learner can now reuse.

## Default Progression

Move from outcome and prerequisites through a sequence of small successful stages, checkpoints, a completed result, and a concise reflection on what the learner can now do.

## Exclude by Default

Do not turn the tutorial into exhaustive reference, troubleshooting coverage, or a menu of alternative approaches.

## Completion Check

A learner following the instructions should reach the promised result, understand the role of each introduced concept, and possess a capability they can apply beyond the example.
